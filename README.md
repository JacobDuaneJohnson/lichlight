# Lichlight

**[▶ Play Lichlight](https://gamedevjake.github.io/lichlight/)**

Current version: **v0.7.12** · [What's new](CHANGELOG.md) · [All releases](https://github.com/gamedevjake/lichlight/releases)

Lichlight is a retro pixel-art dungeon crawler: Zelda-style top-down action meets Diablo 2-style loot. Beneath the drowned city of Vaelmoor, King Oswin Hollowmere sold his soul to the Pale Choir. Now the Bone King waits below.

Fight your way down endless, randomly generated crypt floors in near-total darkness. You'll face 30 kinds of monsters, plus tougher champions, and a boss guards the stairs every 5th floor. There are 20 bosses, from Mother Murk on floor 5 to Oswin, the Bone King, on floor 100, and after that they return as stronger "+1", "+2" versions. Collect gear in four rarities (white normal, blue magic, yellow rare, gold unique) and hunt the named uniques, each with its own lore. The first boss drops the Lich Tome. Torn spell pages, found in cracked stone, pots, locked chests, and on champions, write the spells back in: Fireball, Whirlwind, Frost Nova, Spectral Clone, Ethereal Shield, Ethereal Shift, and Hellmouth.

## How to load the game

The game runs in your web browser. There's nothing to install.

- **Phone (recommended):** open the [game link](https://gamedevjake.github.io/lichlight/) in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and turn your phone sideways. It plays in landscape.
- **iPhone fullscreen:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and launch Lichlight from the new icon. If you don't hear sound, tap the screen once.
- **Android fullscreen:** tap the ⛶ button at the top of the screen, or use Chrome's menu and choose **Add to Home screen**.
- **Computer:** open the link in any modern browser. Mouse controls work like Diablo 2.

Your progress saves automatically each time you reach a new floor. Choose **Continue** on the title screen to resume. When you die, **New Game** throws the run away. **Resurrect** shows up only when you are carrying a Necro Potion; it spends one and wakes you on the same stone at half life. Saves are stored in your browser, so clearing site data wipes them.

## How to play

Walk onto the stairs to go deeper. Gold, potions, keys, and torches are picked up automatically. Gear has to be clicked or tapped to pick it up. Newly looted items are marked **NEW** in your bag.

- **Light:** you start with only a faint glow. Pick up torches and press **F** (**USE** on a phone) to light one. Each torch burns for about 170 seconds, and when it dies you stay in the dark until you light another with **F**. Some unique rings give off magic light of their own. Wall torches and floor braziers start cold. An unlit brazier gives off no light and does not glow in the dark. Light one with your torch and it stays lit, so you can mark the way back.
- **Chests and keys:** locked chests hold magic and rare gear. Open one by clicking it or pressing **F** / **USE** next to it (walking into it does nothing). Keys are hidden under cracked floor tiles and behind cracked brick walls (hit them to break them open), and champions sometimes drop one.
- **Secret passages:** some cracked walls hide a tunnel that links two rooms far apart on the floor, a real shortcut. Both ends are sealed with cracked stone, so break either side to open it. Map Fragments show them on the map.
- **Ghosts:** ghosts and other spirits can drift through walls, but they can't see through them. Break line of sight and they lose you.
- **Loot:** champions, bosses, and chests drop gold, and not every time. Ordinary monsters drop potions and the occasional normal item. Only bosses drop uniques.
- **Level requirements:** every piece of gear has a required level. Gear you are too low to wear is tinted red in your bag, and you can't equip it until you reach that level.
- **Bigger floors, sealed doors:** floors are larger now. Stone doors are sealed by a puzzle in the room (pull levers or touch runes in the order shown on the door, or stand on every pressure plate). Iron gates need a **Gate Key**, which is a separate item from chest keys. The boss room is behind a **blood door** that costs 15% of your max life to open. It never kills you; if you're too low it refuses until you heal.
- **Traps:** narrow corridors can hide pitfalls, dart traps, collapsing ceilings, rolling stones, and fire jets. Each one flashes a warning first, so roll.
- **Torches:** wall torches and floor braziers start out. Walk up with a burning torch and use it (**F** / **USE**) to light them. They stay lit. Hit a wall torch to knock it out. Fireballs, Whirlwind, Frost Nova, and enemy shots put them out too. A burning torch, brazier, sconce, or fire pit relights your own torch. A champion's critical hit can snuff the torch in your hand.
- **Survival:** watch **F**ood, **W**ater, and **R**est. When a bar runs low you move slower. Thirst and exhaustion drain life down to 1. Starvation keeps going: an empty stomach can drop you to 0 and you die. Eat rations from your bag (they turn up in crates, urns, and vaults, or from the trader), drink from stone basins and running water, and **REST** for 1 to 8 hours in a quiet room, a secret chamber, a wall-gap you squeezed through, or a secret passage, with no enemies nearby. 6 to 8 hours is a full rest. Corridors are still too exposed.
- **Crypt shop:** the Hollow Pedlar waits in the boss room, behind a gate. The gate opens about three seconds after the boss falls. There is no shop before the first boss. Sell selected backpack items or **Sell All** (it asks once and shows the gold), repair everything, and buy potions, rations, and Gate Keys.
- **Zoom (computer):** `-` and `=` cycle 0.25x, 0.35x, 0.5x, 0.75x, and 1x. `0` returns to 1x. Darkness, torch light, and fog scale with that view, so unexplored rooms stay black. Phones stay on 1x / 1.15x / 1.25x / 1.35x.
- **Notes:** worn glyph scraps turn up on the floors. They go into **NOTES** in the bag, which is enough to put a prism's sequence in order.
- **Spiked gaps:** a break in a hallway is an open pit of spikes. You walk across it. Dodge-roll still hops it. Standing on the spikes cuts you, and a thought bubble complains. Nothing throws you back.
- **Prisms:** about every 7th floor holds a lich-light prism. The crank is at its base. Scratch the order out of the notebook, and the gate that opens glows the same green as the beam. Solving it spills a soft green light through open doors and casts shadows. The room beyond is a chest of gold.
- **Glimmer pools:** drink once for +25% bonus XP for five minutes. That pool is then dry.
- **Map Fragments:** very rare. Collect 4 and use them from your bag to mark the exit door on the map. The rest of the floor stays dark.
- **Map icons:** the first time you see a puzzle sconce, lever, prism, plaque, crank, glyph socket, secret wall, platform, dark portal, or sink stone, its icon stays on the map (M) for that floor. Pieces you have not seen stay off the map.
- **Satchels and backpacks:** the Bone King and later bosses can drop bags that add 5 or 10 slots (40 max).
- **Drop All:** the **DROP ALL** button in the bag drops every backpack item after you confirm (Shift+click skips the confirm). Equipped gear stays on.
- **Durability and Repair Potions:** weapons wear down as you land hits and armor wears down as you take them. Below 10% the item's icon turns red and you get a warning. At 0 the item shatters and is gone for good. Orange **Repair Potions** fully restore one item: select the potion in your bag and the Character panel opens so you can pick the worn piece. They turn up on the floor, in chests, on champions, and from every boss.

### Touch controls

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Drag anywhere on the left side of the screen (virtual joystick) |
| Attack | Hold **ATTACK** (it aims at the nearest enemy) |
| Dodge roll | **ROLL**: a quick roll that makes you invulnerable while rolling |
| Skills | **FIRE**, **SPIN**, **NOVA**, then **CLONE**, **WARD**, **SHIFT**, and **MAW** once the Lich Tome and that page are yours |
| Pick up gear | Tap the item. Press and hold it to see its stats. |
| Potions | Heart button for life, blue button for mana |
| Use / interact | **USE** button (appears next to chests, doors, levers, runes, water, torches, and the trader; also lights a spare torch in the dark) |
| Map | ⌗ button at the top |
| Inventory | Bag button. Tap an item to see stats and lore, then **EQUIP** or **DROP**. **SORT** organizes your backpack. Rations, Map Fragments, and **REST** are in the bag too. |
| Pause / settings | ❚❚ button at the top (sound, music, battle music, volume, screenshake) |

### Computer controls (Diablo-style)

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | Left-click the ground (hold to keep walking toward the cursor) |
| Attack | Left-click an enemy (hold to keep attacking) |
| Stand in place | Hold **Ctrl**: you won't move. Ctrl + left-click attacks toward the cursor without moving. (Shift no longer does this; Shift+click is a normal click.) |
| Pick up gear / open chest | Left-click it. Hover over an item to see its stats. |
| Cast skill | 1, 2, 3 cast Fireball, Whirlwind, and Frost Nova toward the cursor. 6, 7, 8, 9 cast Spectral Clone, Ethereal Shield, Ethereal Shift, and Hellmouth. Right-click casts the selected skill. Nothing casts until you hold the Lich Tome and that spell's page. |
| Choose right-click skill | F1–F3, or click a skill box on the bottom bar |
| Rebind skill keys | Pause → **SKILL KEYS** (choose which skill each of 1/2/3 and right-click casts) |
| Use / interact | F, or left-click it (chests, doors, levers, runes, water, torches, the trader). In the dark, F lights a spare torch. |
| Eat a ration / rest | R / Z |
| Dodge roll | Space (or K) |
| Potions | Q (life), E (mana) |
| Inventory | I or B |
| Map | M or Tab |

| Pause | Esc or P |
| Optional movement | WASD or arrow keys, J to attack |

## Tips

- Use the roll to dodge through projectiles. You can't be hit mid-roll.
- Watch for red warning markers before a boss's big attacks. Bosses get angrier at half health.
- Save a torch for boss floors. Fighting in the dark is rough.
- Battle music is **Off** by default. Turn it on in the pause menu (**BATTLE MUSIC**) if you want the combat track.
- **LIGHTING: HIGH / LOW** in the pause menu sets the lighting detail. High gives smoother shadows. Low is lighter on older phones and is the default on touch screens.
- Heal up before you open a blood door, and rest before a boss if you can.
- Compare gear in the inventory before equipping. The detail panel shows how it stacks up against what you're wearing.
