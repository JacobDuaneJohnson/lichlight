# Lichlight

**[▶ Play Lichlight](https://jacobduanejohnson.github.io/lichlight/)**

Lichlight is a retro pixel-art dungeon crawler: Zelda-style top-down action meets Diablo 2-style loot. Beneath the drowned city of Vaelmoor, King Oswin Hollowmere sold his soul to the Pale Choir. Now the Bone King waits below.

Fight your way down endless, randomly generated, torchlit crypt floors. You'll face slimes, bats, skeletons, Pale Choir cultists, and tougher champion monsters. Every 5th floor holds Oswin, the Bone King, who blocks the stairs until he falls. Collect gear in four rarities (white normal, blue magic, yellow rare, gold unique), each with random stats. You can also hunt down 21 named unique items, each with its own lore. Level up and unlock Fireball, Whirlwind, and Frost Nova.

## How to load the game

The game runs in your web browser. There's nothing to install.

- **Phone (recommended):** open the [game link](https://jacobduanejohnson.github.io/lichlight/) in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and turn your phone sideways. It plays in landscape.
- **iPhone fullscreen:** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and launch Lichlight from the new icon. If you don't hear sound, tap the screen once.
- **Android fullscreen:** tap the ⛶ button at the top of the screen, or use Chrome's menu and choose **Add to Home screen**.
- **Computer:** open the link in any modern browser.

Your progress saves automatically each time you reach a new floor. Choose **Continue** on the title screen to resume. Dying ends the run and deletes the save. Saves are stored in your browser, so clearing site data wipes them.

## How to play

Walk onto the stairs to go deeper. Loot is picked up automatically when you walk over it.

### Touch controls

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Drag anywhere on the left side of the screen (virtual joystick) |
| Attack | Hold **ATTACK** (it aims at the nearest enemy) |
| Dodge roll | **ROLL**: a quick roll that makes you invulnerable while rolling |
| Skills | **FIRE** (level 1), Whirlwind (level 2), Frost Nova (level 4) |
| Potions | Heart button for life, blue button for mana |
| Inventory | Bag button. Tap an item to see stats and lore, then **EQUIP** or **DROP**. **SORT** organizes your backpack. |
| Pause / settings | ❚❚ button at the top (sound, music, volume) |

### Keyboard controls

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Attack | Space or J |
| Dodge roll | Shift or K |
| Skills | 1, 2, 3 |
| Potions | Q (life), E (mana) |
| Inventory | I |
| Pause | Esc |

## Tips

- Use the roll to dodge through projectiles. You can't be hit mid-roll.
- Gold-named uniques are rare, and you're more likely to find ones you haven't seen yet.
- Compare gear in the inventory before equipping. The detail panel shows how it stacks up against what you're wearing.
